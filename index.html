Frequently Asked Questions

How is my savings calculated?

The app compares what you paid for fuel in the U.S. (converted to CAD using the exchange rate) against what the same amount of fuel would have cost at the Canadian price per liter. Tolls and any extra fuel are then subtracted to give your net savings.

What is the card fee setting?

Your card or bank adds a markup on foreign currency purchases. Set your percentage in Settings → Currency (presets are 3% for USD cards and 5.5% for CAD cards, or enter your own) so your exchange rate reflects what you actually pay.

How do I enter the price per liter?

Enter it in dollars, for example 188.9 cents per liter is entered as 1.889. Don't include a $ symbol in any field.

How do I import trips from a spreadsheet?

Open the menu and tap Import from CSV. The in-app guide includes a downloadable template, a column-by-column reference, and formatting rules.

How do I back up my data?

Open the menu and tap Export to CSV. You can save the file to Files, email it to yourself, or share it anywhere. The exported file can be re-imported later with Import from CSV.

How does syncing work?

Your trips sync automatically across your devices through your own private iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on each device and that iCloud is enabled for the app. Check Settings → Backup for sync status.

My data isn't showing up on a new device.

Confirm you're signed in to the same Apple ID, you're connected to the internet, and iCloud Drive is on. Initial sync can take a few minutes. If it still doesn't appear, email me and I'll help.
