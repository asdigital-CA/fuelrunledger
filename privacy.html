Privacy Policy

Fuel Run Ledger · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Fuel Run Ledger ("the app") is designed to keep your data private. I do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. I have no servers, no accounts, and no way to see the data you enter.

Data you enter

The trip information you enter (dates, fuel amounts and costs, exchange rates, tolls, trip times, and settings) is stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, this data is synced between your own devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) in your private iCloud database. This data is tied to your Apple ID and is protected by Apple. It is not accessible to me or to any third party.

Exchange rates

To fill in the current USD to CAD exchange rate, the app requests rate data from a public exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com). This request contains no personal information and no data from your trips. As with any internet request, that service may see your device's IP address.

What I do not collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contact information, accounts, or logins
  • No data shared with third parties

CSV import and export

When you export a CSV file, it is created on your device and you choose where it goes (Files, Mail, etc.). When you import a CSV, the file is read only on your device. I never receive these files.

Feedback emails

If you choose "Send Feedback," the app opens your email app with a message addressed to me. I only receive what you choose to send. I use your email only to reply to you.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual trips inside the app, or delete the app to remove local data. To remove data synced to iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device and delete the app's data.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
